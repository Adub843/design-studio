Questions and answers

Can't find yours? Email hello@charleston3d.com.

What is EPIX3D?

A set of online makers for 3D prints: light boxes, keychains, name tags, signs, lithophanes, license plate frames, nameplates, hype chains and photo-to-3D models. You design in your browser and see the result in 3D, then order a print made in Charleston, SC, or download the files with Platinum.

Is it free?

Designing, saving up to 50 projects and ordering prints are free. Each printed piece is quoted when you order. Platinum, which adds 3MF and STL downloads, is $15 a month.

How does ordering a print work?

Press Order a print in any maker. We keep a copy of your print file and send you to checkout on the Charleston3D store with your design as a free print request. We look it over and reply with a price, usually within 1-2 business days. You pay nothing until you accept it.

The License Plate Maker can also show a fixed price when one is listed in the store.

Where are the prints made?

In Charleston, SC. Orders and Platinum check out on the Charleston3D Shopify store, where EPIX3D is sold.

Do I need an account?

Not to try the makers. You need a free account to save to My projects and to order a print. Without one, some makers keep a draft in your browser.

Which files can Platinum members download?

3MF files with one part per colour, ready for Bambu Studio, plus STL files for any slicer such as Orca, Prusa or Cura. Each maker's print check tells you which printer beds the design fits.

How does Platinum get turned on?

Start from Go Platinum on the pricing page while you're signed in. When Shopify confirms the payment, downloads turn on for 35 days from that payment, and each monthly renewal extends them.

Can I use logos, team names or brand names?

Only if you have the right to. We won't print trademarks or artwork you don't own, and some makers block well-known names on their own.
