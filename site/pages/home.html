How it works

From idea to a finished print

Three steps, and you see exactly what you will get before you pay for anything.

  1. Design it

    Type your words, drop in a picture, pick your filament colours. Every maker shows a live preview and checks that it will print.

  2. Save it

    Make a free account and keep up to 50 projects. Open one later from any device and pick up where you left off.

  3. Print it

    Order a print and we make it in Charleston, SC. Or go Platinum to download the 3MF and STL files for your own printer.

Good to know

What does it cost to use the makers?

Designing, saving and ordering are free. A printed piece is quoted for each order. Platinum, for downloading your files, is $15 a month.

Where are prints made?

In Charleston, SC. Orders check out on the Charleston3D store, where EPIX3D prints are sold.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The makers run in your browser on a computer, tablet or phone. A computer gives you the most room to work.
