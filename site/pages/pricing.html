Questions about Platinum

How do I pay?

Platinum checks out on the Charleston3D Shopify store, where EPIX3D is sold. Shopify handles the payment; we never see your card.

When do downloads turn on?

As soon as the payment goes through, usually within a minute. Use the same email as your EPIX3D account, or start from the Go Platinum button while signed in so we can match it.

What if I stop paying?

Downloads stay on until the end of the month you paid for. Your saved projects stay in your account, and you can still order prints.

How much does a printed piece cost?

Each print is quoted when you order, because size, colours and detail change the cost. You pay nothing until you accept the price.
