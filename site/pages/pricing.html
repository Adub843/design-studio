How print prices work

The price in each maker changes as you design. It comes from the filament your design uses and the time it takes to print, worked out from the print file itself.

  • FilamentBigger, taller and more solid designs use more. Each extra colour adds a little for colour changes.
  • Print timeCharged by the hour the printer runs, plus power and machine wear.
  • MinimumPrints start at $18. Orders are rounded up to the whole dollar.
  • ShippingAdded at checkout on the Charleston3D store, with tax.

Questions about plans

How do I pay?

Prints, Platinum and Design files all check out on the Charleston3D Shopify store, where EPIX3D is sold. Shopify handles the payment; we never see your card.

How much does a printed piece cost?

Each maker shows the price as you design, starting at $18. It's checked again from your print file when you order. Very large prints over $500 go to checkout as a free request, and we reply with a price.

When does Export turn on?

As soon as the payment goes through, usually within a minute. Use the same email as your EPIX3D account, or start from the Go Platinum button while signed in so we can match it.

Are Platinum and Design files the same plan?

No. They're separate monthly plans: Platinum is for exporting 3D print files from the makers, Design files is for the SVG, PNG and DTF art library. Join either one, or both.

What if I stop paying?

Your plan stays on until the end of the month you paid for. Your saved projects stay in your account, and you can still order prints.
