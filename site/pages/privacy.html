Privacy

Short and plain. Last updated 7 November 2026.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address and a scrambled form of your password (we can't read it), the date you joined and whether you have Platinum.
  • Your projects: the designs you save, including any pictures you put in them, and a small preview picture.
  • Your print orders: the print file and preview made when you press Order a print, so we can make it.
  • A sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Short-lived counters of failed sign-in attempts, to stop people guessing passwords.

What we don't keep

Payment details. Orders and Platinum check out on the Charleston3D Shopify store, so your card is handled by Shopify and never reaches us. Shopify's own privacy policy covers checkout.

Why

Only to run the site: to sign you in, keep your projects, print your orders and know whether downloads are on for you. We don't sell or share your information, and we don't send marketing emails from EPIX3D.

Where it lives

On Cloudflare, which hosts the site. Order details you enter at checkout are kept by Shopify.

Removing your data

You can delete any project yourself in My projects. To close your account and remove everything, email hello@charleston3d.com from the address on your account.

Questions

Email hello@charleston3d.com. EPIX3D is run from Charleston, SC.
