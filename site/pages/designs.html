    How it works

    1. JoinCheckout runs on the Charleston3D Shopify store. Use the email on your EPIX3D account.
    2. Search and downloadFind a design by name or type and download the file. Up to 60 a day.
    3. Make itCut it, press it or print it. New designs show up as they're added.
    Is this the same as Platinum?

    No. Design files is its own $15 monthly plan for the art library. Platinum is for exporting 3D print files from the makers.

    What can I make with the files?

    Shirts, tumblers, mugs, signs and other pieces for yourself, as gifts or to sell. Please don't resell or share the files themselves.

    Why is the library sometimes offline?

    The files are served from our own studio library. If it's briefly offline, try again a little later; your plan isn't affected.
